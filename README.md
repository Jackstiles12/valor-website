## Coverage and verification
All 7 active listings were captured, matching the seller profile count and the Available & in stock filter. All 54 photographs exposed for those listings were downloaded and each file was validated as an image. Pending filter returned “We didn't find anything”: 0 pending.
The unfiltered seller history was scrolled to its end: 141 dashboard entries (7 active, 126 sold, 8 out of stock). Counts are listing entries, not unique physical items; apparent duplicates are preserved. The oldest entries include legacy group listings attributed to Mark Rosenthal; ownership is not independently inferred from their presence in the dashboard.

## Import
Use active_inventory.csv for your live website: seven rows with titles, current asking prices, descriptions, stated specifications, condition, availability, categories, direct Facebook links and relative photo filenames. Multiple photo filenames are separated with |. Prices are plain USD numbers. Unknown fields are blank. Categories are editorial groupings based on the listing text, not claims of Facebook's assigned category.
inventory.csv contains those seven plus eight recent sold listings; sold items are explicitly unavailable. pending_inventory.csv is header-only. Historical status indexes are separate and must not be imported as available inventory. The historical archive is an index, not a complete photo/description/link export of all sold listings. Some historical vehicle descriptions/specifications remain blank in the detailed CSV.
