Our foundation

Meet the Founder

Jack Stiles | Founder, Valor Powersports, Equipment & Logistics

I'm Jack Stiles, a United States Marine Corps veteran and the founder of Valor, based in Gurley, Alabama. I served from 2018 to 2022 as an 0811 artillery cannoneer and artillery section chief, leaving the Marine Corps as a corporal. My time in uniform taught me the importance of discipline, accountability, teamwork and keeping your word.

Outside of business, I enjoy the outdoors, working with equipment and spending time with my family. My faith and family are at the heart of what I want to build. Valor brings together my passion for powersports, machinery and transportation with my desire to build something meaningful from the ground up.

Life beyond the business

My dream. Our future.

My vision is to grow Valor from a local pre-owned equipment business into a trusted dealership offering both new and used powersports and equipment, alongside a professional logistics operation serving the Southeast. I want to build a business that creates opportunities, earns lasting customer relationships and remains grounded in the values it started with.
